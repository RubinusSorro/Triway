# What Makes TRIWAY Different
TRIWAY is built for one vehicle type, in one city, so the interface is organized around routes rather than a menu of services.
- **Local focus:** only tricycle rides, cash payment, Antipolo wording (demo names pending confirmation).
- **Route-first home:** a destination search plus route "tickets" with a dotted fare stub. There is no service grid.
- **Booking:** a single flow with a One-way or Quick Return choice. Quick Return is one booking with two legs and one total.
- **Role-specific IA:** riders see Home, Routes, Trips, Profile. Drivers see Home, Requests, History, Profile, and are gated by verification.
- **Shape and type:** ticket-shaped route cards, pill buttons, Inter at 800 for titles, 4px rhythm.
- **Brand:** the approved green palette is kept. Originality comes from structure and components, not from changing color.
## Principles comparison (general, not a critique of any product)
| Principle | General multi-service apps | TRIWAY |
|---|---|---|
| Entry point | Choose a service | Choose a route |
| Scope | Many services | One vehicle, one city |
| Payment | Mixed | Cash only (assumed) |
| Trip model | Single trip | One-way or one booking with two legs |
Limit: no side-by-side visual audit against competitor screens was done.
