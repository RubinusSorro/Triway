# User flows
Rider: Splash > Role > Login > Home > Routes or Search > Book > Review > Matching > Assigned > Tracking > In progress > Completed > Receipt > Rating.
Quick Return: one booking, Leg 1 outbound and Leg 2 return, one total, same flow.
Driver: Login > Documents > Verification (pending/rejected/approved) > Home (online) > Request (15s countdown) > Accepted > Navigate > Arrived > Start > Complete > Cash confirmation.
Failure branches: no driver, cancelled, booking failed, network, location denied, driver offline.
Matching, movement and countdown are simulated in the previews.
